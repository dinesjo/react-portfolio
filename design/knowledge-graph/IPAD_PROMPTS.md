# Photorealistic MAZE iPad cover

The built-in ImageGen tool recreated the approved pencil cover’s MAZE example
in a truck workshop. The iPad screen is a reconstruction from the portfolio
artwork. Each version keeps the visible question, verdict, Evidence button, and
composer.

## Landscape

[master-thesis-ipad-wide.png](../../src/assets/project-originals/master-thesis-ipad-wide.png) (1536 × 1024)

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: 3:2 landscape master’s thesis cover for a computer science portfolio.
Image 1 is the original pencil cover. Use it as the authoritative reference for the visible MAZE conversation and for the relationship between the engineer, device, and complete truck. Reimagine that moment as an entirely photorealistic candid editorial photograph in a working European truck test bay.
Frame from just behind and beside an anonymous engineer in a slightly worn navy work jacket, the shoulder and partial back of the head at the left margin, turned toward a real 11-inch iPad held in portrait orientation near the edge of a workshop bench. The hand grips the lower corner naturally. The iPad is clearly the foreground subject, slim dark aluminum, rounded corners, black bezel and plausible 4:3 screen proportions. Angle it only a few degrees so the whole screen and device are visible, in sharp focus around the middle of the frame. Keep the iPad at human scale, a tool the engineer can take to the vehicle. One complete unbranded white European cab-over tractor truck with cab, chassis, and wheels assembled is parked just beyond, visible at right through the workshop bay, comfortably inside frame and softly out of focus. Lived-in but tidy, with concrete, muted amber safety markings, painted steel, realistic indirect daylight, subdued warm shadows. Natural colors, a quiet documentary feel, no advertising treatment.
On the iPad render the original MAZE UI from image 1 adapted to the tablet viewport. Clean white canvas, small right-aligned rounded question bubble, lightly outlined white answer panel below, small rounded Evidence button at its lower right, ample white space, bottom rounded composer and circular send arrow. Preserve the original words exactly: “Can I use FPC448 J with FPC552 DQ?” “This configuration is BLOCKED.” “Evidence” “Ask about MAZE...” Set BLOCKED in bold, with the original understated coral underline. Use thin muted neutral strokes for the secondary explanation, as in the reference. Use credible compact browser-app typography with the text in focus and legible. No new words, charts, metrics, top logos, recording UI, desktop monitor, keyboard, extra device, floating graphics, or painterly textures. Keep the display and truck within the central 85% of the canvas. Finished landscape photograph, edge to edge.
```
