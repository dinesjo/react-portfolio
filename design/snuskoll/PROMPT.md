# SnusKoll catalog refresh

The [updated cover](../../src/assets/project-originals/snuskoll-catalog-refresh.png) shows the signed-out [SnusKoll catalog](https://snuskoll.dinesjo.se/snus-database) as viewed on 7 November 2026. Built-in ImageGen recreated the display from a Browser capture. The edited pixels were clipped to the webpage area in the [original cover](../../src/assets/project-originals/snuskoll.png), preserving the browser toolbar, MacBook, and room.

```text
Use case: compositing.
Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET, an existing photorealistic MacBook on an oak table. Image 2 is the exact replacement webpage, a screenshot of SnusKoll’s current signed-out Swedish Snusdatabas page. Replace only the outdated webpage pixels below the MacBook's browser toolbar with image 2, projected exactly into the existing display bounds. Preserve image 1's pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, camera view, laptop position, dark browser toolbar, bezel, keyboard, trackpad, mug, table, books, furnishings, background and light. Treat all pixels outside the webpage area as locked. Do not zoom, crop, reframe, restyle, or redraw the photo. The new webpage should reproduce image 2 faithfully with its original typography, icons, colors, values, five filters including Smakprofil, and product card order Knox, Göteborgs Rapé, LD. Use the screenshot as a flattened insert so small Swedish labels and details are reproduced exactly. Maintain the realistic appearance of a bright laptop screen. Return the whole photograph.
```
